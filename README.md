I live where people, systems, and information meet. Before teaching, I spent about eight years in industry, including Suncor, Facebook, and seven years at Palantir, helping organizations change through data and machine-learning systems.

I spent much of that time teaching machines to learn from data. Humans require a few more inputs; now I guide people in learning how to learn, teach one another, and teach machines. I care about making useful knowledge understandable, reusable, and freely available.

Somewhere along the way I left the sensible path after Suncor and Facebook to build a startup. It did not become the company we imagined, but we did manage to sell what we built for a profit. I also ended up with two U.S. patents and a University of Waterloo teaching award. The details are in LinkedIn and my CV.
