I live where people, systems, and information meet. Before teaching, I spent about eight years in industry, including seven years at Palantir. That meant working in fast-moving implementation teams, collaborating with product teams to improve the tools, and, most importantly, learning from very smart end users to help organizations change how they worked.

Much of that involved teaching machines to learn from data. Humans require a few more inputs; now I guide people to learn by doing, learn from one another, and, eventually, teach machines themselves. I care about making useful knowledge understandable, reusable, and freely available.

Before Palantir, after co-ops at Suncor and Facebook, I dropped out of Waterloo to build a startup. We eventually sold what we built, and that winding path ultimately led me to Palantir. Along the way I also ended up with two U.S. patents and a University of Waterloo teaching award. The details are in LinkedIn and my CV.
