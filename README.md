I live where people, systems, and information meet. Before teaching, I spent about eight years in industry: roughly a year interning, including at Suncor and Facebook, followed by seven years at Palantir. There I worked with fast-moving implementation teams, collaborated with product teams to improve the tools, and, most importantly, learned from very smart end users to help organizations change how they operated.

Much of that involved teaching machines to learn from data. Humans require a few more inputs; now I guide people to learn by doing, learn from one another, and eventually teach machines themselves. I care about making useful knowledge understandable, reusable, and freely available.

After internships at Suncor and Facebook, I dropped out of Waterloo Engineering to build a startup. We eventually sold what we built. Somehow, I made it back, graduated without losing time, and that winding path ultimately led me to Palantir. Along the way I also ended up with two U.S. patents and a University of Waterloo teaching award. The details are in LinkedIn and my CV.
